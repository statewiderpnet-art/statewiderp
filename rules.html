Respect the scene

Community rules

These are starter guidelines, not a replacement for your official server rulebook. Update them to match the rules enforced by SWRP staff.

01 / RESPECT

Respect every member

Treat players and staff respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, targeted abuse and disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

02 / ROLEPLAY

Keep roleplay believable

Stay in character during active scenes and avoid actions that deliberately undermine another person's roleplay.

03 / FAIR PLAY

No exploiting or cheating

Do not exploit bugs, use unauthorised cheats or abuse server systems for an unfair advantage.

04 / SCENES

Value the scene

Give other participants a reasonable chance to respond. Avoid random violence, forced outcomes and scene disruption.

05 / STAFF

Follow staff instructions

Cooperate with moderation. If you disagree with a decision, use the official appeal or support process calmly.

06 / REPORTING

Report issues properly

Provide relevant context and evidence through the official support channel. Do not publish private information.

Before launch, publish your complete official rules and make sure they are consistent with your Discord and FiveM server policies.
