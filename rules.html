COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Rules & Expectations

These starter rules establish the basics. Replace or expand them to match the official USRP rulebook before publishing.

01 / Respect

Respect everyone

  • Treat members and staff respectfully.
  • Harassment, hate speech and targeted abuse are not acceptable.
  • Follow Discord platform rules and Roblox/FiveM platform requirements where applicable.
02 / Roleplay

Keep roleplay fair

  • Value realistic, collaborative scenarios over winning.
  • Do not exploit bugs or use unauthorised cheats.
  • Follow server-specific rules for scenes, vehicles and emergency services.
03 / Safety

Protect the community

  • Do not share private information or threaten other members.
  • Use official reporting channels for concerns.
  • Do not impersonate staff or claim permissions you do not have.
04 / Staff decisions

Follow moderation processes

Follow reasonable staff instructions and use the official appeal route if you believe an action was incorrect. Staff should apply rules consistently and professionally.

05 / Important

Official rulebook

This page is a draft template and is not a substitute for your final server rules. Add the approved rules, punishment guidelines and appeal process before launch.
