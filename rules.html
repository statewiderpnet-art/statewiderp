Server Rules

These rules are in place to keep Statewide Roleplay fair, immersive and enjoyable. All members are expected to read and follow them while participating in our community.

Important: Not knowing the rules is not an excuse for breaking them. Staff may take action depending on the seriousness and frequency of an offence.

01. General Conduct

  1. Treat all community members with respect.
  2. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination and targeted abuse are prohibited.
  3. Do not spam, deliberately disrupt discussions or evade moderation.
  4. Follow reasonable instructions from authorised staff.
  5. Do not exploit bugs or deliberately abuse server systems.

02. Roleplay Standards

  1. Remain in character during active roleplay unless staff authorise otherwise.
  2. Do not use information your character did not obtain in-game (metagaming).
  3. Do not perform unrealistic actions that unfairly force outcomes on other players (powergaming).
  4. Do not randomly kill or attack players without a valid roleplay reason (RDM).
  5. Do not use vehicles to intentionally run over or attack players without valid roleplay grounds (VDM).
  6. Value your character's life and respond realistically to serious threats.
  7. Do not leave the game to avoid an ongoing roleplay situation or its consequences.

03. Exploits and Fair Play

  1. Cheats, exploit executors and third-party advantages are prohibited.
  2. Do not abuse glitches to gain money, items, access or an unfair advantage.
  3. Report significant bugs to the staff team rather than exploiting them.
  4. Do not impersonate staff or falsely claim to have permissions.

04. Police, Fire and EMS Roleplay

  1. Respect department procedures and roleplay realistically.
  2. Do not impersonate emergency service personnel to disrupt roleplay.
  3. Do not interfere with an active scene without a valid roleplay reason.
  4. Department members must follow their department's additional policies and training standards.

05. Vehicles and Driving

  1. Drive in a manner appropriate to the situation and your vehicle.
  2. Do not deliberately use vehicles to disrupt scenes or block players without a roleplay reason.
  3. Follow any additional traffic and vehicle regulations set by the server.

06. Staff and Reports

  1. Do not argue with staff to disrupt an active situation. You may use the appropriate appeal process afterwards.
  2. Provide truthful information when submitting a report or appeal.
  3. Do not make false or malicious reports against other members.
  4. Concerns about staff conduct should be raised through the appropriate reporting channel.

07. Discord and Community

  1. Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  2. Keep content in the appropriate channels and follow channel-specific instructions.
  3. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.
  4. Advertising, unsolicited promotions and invite spam are prohibited unless authorised.

08. Enforcement

  1. Rule violations may result in a warning, kick, temporary suspension or permanent ban.
  2. Serious offences may result in immediate action.
  3. Staff will consider the circumstances and severity of each incident.
  4. Appeals may be submitted through the official community channels.
Need help? If you have a question about a rule or believe an action was taken incorrectly, contact the staff team through our official Discord.
